Support

Contact Train Track at support.traintrack@gmail.com for help with Train Track.

Keep a copy of your training

Use the app's export and backup tools to save a copy of your data. Store exported files somewhere you can find them later.

Coach availability

The Coach uses Apple Intelligence on your device. A compatible iPhone and an available on-device model are required.

Apple Health

Health integration is optional. You can manage or withdraw permission through Apple's Health app and iOS Settings.

Report a problem

Include your iPhone model, iOS version, app version, and the steps that led to the problem. Avoid sending private workout exports or progress photos unless you intend to share them.
