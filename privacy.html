Privacy policy

Last updated: 21 October 2026

Developer: Train Track

Your training data

Train Track stores routines, workout logs, settings, measurements, selected photos, saved Coach chats, and local backups in the app's storage on your iPhone. The app has no accounts, advertising, tracking, or analytics.

Apple Health

With your permission, Train Track can read height, weight, and biological sex to fill in your profile. If enabled, it can write completed workouts and estimated active energy to Apple Health. You can withdraw access through Apple Health or iOS Settings.

Photos and notifications

The app receives only photos you select through the system picker. Rest notifications and Live Activities run locally on your device.

The Coach

The Coach uses Apple's on-device language model. Training summaries are calculated and processed on your phone. The developer does not receive your questions, responses, or training data. Apple Intelligence availability and Apple's terms apply.

Exports and backups

Exported files can include workout history, measurements, chats, and selected photos. You choose where to store and share them. Your device may include app data in iPhone or iCloud backups, according to your Apple settings.

Your choices

You can delete individual records, use the app's erase option, or delete the app. Health and notification permissions can be changed in iOS Settings.

This website

This static website does not use analytics, forms, or tracking scripts. When published on GitHub Pages, GitHub processes requests to serve it under its own privacy policy.

Fitness guidance

Train Track provides general fitness tracking and guidance, not medical advice. Consult a qualified professional about medical concerns.
